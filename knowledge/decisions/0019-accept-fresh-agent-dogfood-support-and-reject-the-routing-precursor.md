# Accept fresh-agent dogfood support and reject the routing precursor as posed

Recorded by `agent:claude-opus-5` from the accountable maintainer's disposition.
The semantic choice is the maintainer's; this record is faithful transcription.

## Context

Decision 0018 adopted an evidence-gated capability-evolution method as canonical
Gnostoa-self operating knowledge, and recorded explicitly that the
canonicalization was a **claim** to be falsified or supported by a fresh-agent
continuation test. Work Item #35 had separately selected a routing precursor: the
hypothesis that an already-existing deterministic consistency check could be made
reliably unavoidable **primarily by routing it at the correct boundary**, without
new evidence or validation semantics.

Two fresh agents were then given only the minimal task of proceeding with the
selected routing precursor through the repository's normal routes. Neither
received prior conversation, the expected answer, the C4 history or any
explanation of the method. Both stopped before implementation. The second
reported an entrance-gate disposition refuting the precursor as posed.

The two questions — does the canonical route work, and does the precursor
hold — were answered by the same experiment, and this Decision answers both.

## Decision

**A.** Accept the fresh-agent dogfood evidence as **support** for the
Gnostoa-self canonical discovery, admission and stop-control method.

**B.** Do **not** claim autonomous semantic correctness. The evidence does not
show that a fresh agent reaches correct semantic conclusions unaided.

**C.** **Bounded human semantic review remains materially necessary.** It caught
incorrect surface classification, misreading of a frozen historical control, and
confusion between routing an existing mechanism and inventing new semantics —
each before implementation.

**D.** **Reject the #35 routing precursor as posed.** It is refuted at the
entrance gate, before implementation, and is not to be rescued.

**E.** Preserve the narrow existing-checker finding: once supplied the correct
observation, the existing deterministic mechanism did detect the specific
mismatch. That remains true.

**F.** Record that **routing-only sufficiency was refuted**, because two
boundaries are separately unresolved: how the required observations are
authoritatively derived and bound, and where a non-public review boundary could
make the check unavoidable.

**G.** **Select and activate no successor mechanism.**

**H.** The method remains **Gnostoa-self only** and its lifecycle record remains
`status: draft`. No generic or public promotion occurs.

This Decision does not amend Decision 0018's historical choice, does not amend or
renumber Decision 0016's increment sequence, and does not activate increment 2.

## Consequences

- The capability loop behaved as intended: a smaller precursor was selected, and
  entrance-gate evidence falsified it **before** any mechanism was built or
  retained. That is evidence for the method, not evidence that the underlying
  failed property is mitigated.
- **The original failed property remains unmitigated:** critical workflow
  constraints are advisory rather than mechanically enforced.
- A negative entrance-gate result is a complete and valid experimental outcome.
  Nothing is owed in response to it.
- Two unresolved boundaries are now named rather than solved. Naming them selects
  nothing; any future work on either requires its own research, selection and
  admission.
- The dogfood evidence is Gnostoa-on-Gnostoa. Both agents operated on this
  project, so this establishes no transfer to an independently owned project;
  that remains future B3 evidence.
- The claim recorded by Decision 0018 is now **partially supported and still
  open**, which is the honest state: supported for discovery and stop behaviour,
  unestablished for semantic correctness.
