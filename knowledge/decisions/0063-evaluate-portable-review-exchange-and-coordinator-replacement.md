# Evaluate portable review exchange and coordinator replacement

## Context

The owner accepted the linked concrete scope and budget. Work Item #233 records
the separate selection, initial behavior map, base and proposed changed paths
before implementation. This agent-authored Decision records that choice; it is
not human approval of the resulting candidate or stable knowledge promotion.

The study found repeated manual assignment/review transfer and task
reconstruction. Existing D11-F1 evidence concerns acceptance logic, not actual
transport or recovery. The next question is whether durable assignments and
individual review results survive a coordinator replacement without owner relay.

## Reuse and bounded choice

Use existing Python file, JSON and process primitives and qualified official
execution interfaces. Add only experimental assignment/result binding and
checkpoint glue under `tests/fixtures/review_exchange/`. The dated research
compared Agent Deck, Agent Orchestrator and larger durable workflow systems;
their broader scheduling/service machinery is not needed for this two-job,
single-host experiment. No code or dependency is imported from those projects.
Reconsider the residual choice if the admitted setup budget is exceeded.

Gnostoa retains candidate, assignment, review and disposition meanings. Native
tool outputs remain replaceable inputs; terminal success is not approval.
Native responses are retained before normalization. Existing tool licensing
does not establish subscription entitlement or permission for a deployment.

## Decision

1. Construct one **normal, self-only experimental/test** candidate. Limit changes
   to the fixture, its test, this Decision, assessment/native evidence and index.
   No public schema, supported CLI, policy, production service or protected
   acceptance principal is introduced.
2. Freeze eligible assignments independently of dispatch records and preserve
   exact reference candidate `6ee2deb9584ebb9a8f4fa4076fef10f9500903b9`.
   Keep original reviews/recommendations separate from our dispositions. Missing,
   ambiguous, stale and dissenting information must remain visible.
3. Before the corrected controller, retain independently derived expectations
   and execute an intentionally defective transport counterexample. An absent
   executable or parser failure is infrastructure failure, not behavioral RED.
   Replay the frozen oracle and a discriminating mutation after implementation.
4. Controlled subprocesses first test valid exchange, single-agent operation,
   coordinator replacement, never-dispatched work, ambiguous dispatch, stale
   subjects, duplicate results, dissent and retention before normalization.
   They do not establish real-runtime portability or hostile-process isolation.
5. Attempt at most two live reviewer assignments and one coordinator handoff
   only through qualified official personal routes. Use public input, read-only
   reference source and no GitHub mutation within the trial. Retain the outputs
   externally to the coordinator. Missing entitlement, uncertain paid usage or
   unavailable access leaves that integration unestablished.
6. Enforce the selected effort boundary: two hours adapter/setup effort, at most
   45 minutes live execution, one review round and no automatic reviewer retry.
   No additional paid API spend is allowed. Record account-specific usage limits
   and unknown accounting honestly; one assignment need not equal one request.
7. Record owner relay/reconstruction, interruptions, setup, repair, latency and
   usage separately. No retrospective time-saving percentage or general quality
   equivalence follows from this small feasibility trial. Finish with one
   use/narrow/abandon recommendation and an exact-candidate human review packet.

## Consequences

Local durable files and process replacement can establish the tested handoff
behavior, not crash consistency under every failure, authenticated evidence,
exclusive acceptance authority, semantic completeness or L10 closure. A
single-agent route is valid assistance but not independent review. Unavailable
live routes and negative outcomes are retained without mechanism expansion.

Ordinary branch, draft PR and evidence publication are separate delivery
activities. Merge, adoption and public-contract promotion need their own owner
acts. Related #3/#10/#11/#201 work is not automatically admitted by this slice.
