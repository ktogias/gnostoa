## Decision

The ordinary controller owns a durable, level-triggered `PROJECT_PROGRESS`
obligation for the Work Item or selected objective after terminal reconciliation
and on a fresh selected idle start. A completed PR supplies evidence, never a new
execution target or automatic closure of broader scope.

Account for progress only through an exact designated next owner or started
operation, a real owner-bound gate with a supported durable resume selector,
independent work permitted by the existing WIP/dependency judgment, or complete
current scope-bound evidence establishing quiescence. Missing or stale evidence
retains the obligation and causes bounded refresh. Empty assignments and prose
cannot discharge it. Accounted waits and legitimate quiescence remain distinct
from incomplete evidence.

One private neutral implementation owns progress transitions and typed result
validation. The actual pilot controller and intake/egress consumers call it through
small native/provider adapters; remove or deactivate the old local bodies rather
than retaining a fallback engine. The core consumes opaque scope and operation
identities and normalized source, owner and outcome facts. Adapters retain native
statuses, exact gate subjects, provider acquisition, freshness translation,
eligibility, storage, locks and external effects.

Consume the existing generic operation identity, ingress/outbox, publication
reconciliation and checkpoint recovery. Persist intent before egress; preserve the
same logical operation across restart, role switch and quota reset. Reconcile an
uncertain effect before retry. No arbitrary exactly-once side-effect guarantee is
made. The normal path requests progress in the current or next eligible controller
cycle; missed events converge within two successful, complete eligible observations.
Real provider, source, quota and human holds retain their actual resume conditions
and are reported separately from avoidable scheduler delay.

Admission and WIP semantics remain with their existing #6/Router/native judgment
owner. A `CURRENT` provider retrieval is evidence, not admission. This slice creates
no admission evaluator, canonical ready queue, public API/schema, general workflow
engine, credential policy or merge authority. Decisions 0112, 0133 and 0135 retain
exact-subject merge verdict, coalescing and publication readback responsibilities.

The implementation remains a private self-hosted capability with one active owner;
its adapter seam adds maintenance and must be verified at actual runtime consumers.
Source freshness cannot solve semantic admission or human-review oracle limits.

exact-head review and a real unnudged successor launch remain distinct evidence
obligations. No public promotion or broader orchestration engine follows from this
